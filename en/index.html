Private, encrypted, no internet

Your whole family's health, only on your phone

Medipop is a private notebook for the illnesses, visits, treatments and vaccinations of the people and pets in your home. What you write stays on your phone, encrypted.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

What it does

  • No internet, no account

    The app does not connect to the internet and has no account and no server. No ads, no usage analytics, no tracking.

  • Encrypted, only on your phone

    The database is encrypted with AES-256. The key is protected by the phone and by a recovery code that only you have: not even the developer can read your data.

  • People and pets

    A profile for each, with a family role. Vaccinations, illnesses, visits and treatments, with "Repeat treatment" so you do not retype everything. For dogs and cats, from version 1.1, the microchip number can be read with the camera from the pet passport or from the barcode label.

  • Medical certificates, from version 1.1

    Sport, sick leave, school absence, fitness: take a photo of the pages or import a PDF, and they stay on the phone, encrypted. The app reads the text on the phone, without internet, and suggests the type and the dates: you confirm them.

  • Cards, from version 1.1

    Health card, insurance cards, pharmacy cards: you take a photo of both sides and Medipop reads the details and the barcode on the phone, without internet. It saves them straight away and asks you to check them. At the counter you tap "Show the code" and the barcode comes back up, large, on the screen.

  • Prescriptions, from version 1.2

    From "Add to the diary", "Prescription": you take a photo of the pages or import a PDF. Medipop reads the text and the barcodes on the phone, without internet, and suggests the date, the doctor, the prescription number, the validity and the medicines it recognises: nothing is added until you confirm. The barcode can be shown again, large, on the screen with "Show the code". From a prescribed medicine, "Create the treatment" opens a treatment already filled in with that medicine: you enter the dose and the times, Medipop does not work them out. The prescription can be shared as a PDF. It is a personal copy: whether a pharmacy accepts a copy depends on the country and on the kind of prescription.

  • Medicine cabinet, from version 1.2

    In "More", "Medicine cabinet" you keep the medicine packs you have at home. For each one you enter the medicine, whose it is (the whole family, or one person or pet), the expiry date, the lot, how much is left, where you keep it and, if you like, photos (up to six: for example the front and the side with the expiry date). You can add one from one or more photos: Medipop reads on the phone the name on the front of the pack, the printed code (the small square code or, in Italy, the pharmacy sticker), the expiry date and the lot; you check before saving. From "Add to the diary", "Medicine purchased" follows the same steps: when you save, the pack goes into the "Medicine cabinet" and the purchase into the diary (date, pharmacy, price). The list shows expired packs first, then those expiring within 30 days, which also appear in the "Today" tab, under "Expiring". You get one notice 30 days before the expiry and one on the day itself, with a generic text that never names the medicine; you can switch it off for each pack. From a pack you tap "Start a treatment".

  • Package leaflet, from version 1.2

    Medipop contains no leaflets and has no internet. For a pack whose code belongs to Italy, Switzerland, Spain, France or to a medicine authorised for the whole EU, the "Package leaflet (official site)" button opens the phone's browser on the official site of the medicines agency (AIFA, Swissmedic's medicine information, AEMPS CIMA, the French public medicines database, EMA). Before opening it, Medipop tells you that site will see the page asked for and your IP address, like any site: Medipop sends nothing else. For German and Austrian codes there is no button.

  • Sharing between parents

    You export an encrypted file, send it with "Share" and the other parent opens it with "Open with Medipop". From version 1.1 the file can also carry the photos of certificates and cards; from version 1.2 also prescriptions and the packs of the medicine cabinet. It works between iPhone and Android too.

  • Just one child, not the whole household, from version 1.1

    If the parents do not live together, you create a "circle" to share only part of the family. You choose whom to share, in any combination: just one child, two people, even just the dog. Each circle has its own code: whoever receives it opens only the backups made for that circle and sees nothing of the rest of the family. From version 1.2, a pack in the medicine cabinet that belongs to the whole family is never in a backup made for a circle; the pack of a person or pet in the circle is.

  • Medicine reminders

    You choose the time to be reminded of a medicine. You also choose which family members you are reminded for. A notification never shows the medicine, at most the family member's first name, and you can hide it. From version 1.2 a notice also tells you about medicines in the medicine cabinet that are about to expire, without naming the medicine.

  • Five languages

    Italian, English, French, Spanish and German. App lock with fingerprint or the phone's PIN.

A look at the app

How it protects your data

  • No account, no server, no ads, no usage analytics, no tracking.
  • No internet access: the app does not even ask for the permission to connect.
  • Package leaflets are not in the app: if you want one, the "Package leaflet (official site)" button opens the phone's browser on the official site of the medicines agency, outside Medipop. The app itself stays without internet access.
  • The database is encrypted with AES-256. The key is protected by the phone and by a recovery code that only you have: the developer cannot read your data.
  • Photos of certificates, cards, prescriptions and packs are cleaned of hidden data (location, phone model) and encrypted one by one. Text and barcodes are read on the phone, without internet.
  • Encrypted backups (.medipop files) that you save or send wherever you like, to change phone or to exchange data with the other parent.

If you lose the recovery code and the phone, the data is lost for good. That is why the app asks you to keep the code somewhere safe on first launch.

It is not a medical device

Medipop only stores what you type and, from version 1.1, the photos of the pages of medical certificates and of cards; from version 1.2 also the pages of prescriptions and the photos of packs. It does not calculate doses, check interactions, say what a medicine is for, give medical advice or diagnose, and it is not a medical device: it does not replace your doctor or vet. You cannot save other photos or documents. Medipop contains no package leaflets: the "Package leaflet (official site)" button opens the phone's browser on the official site of the medicines agency, outside the app. The photo of a card is a handy copy: it does not replace the card where the chip or the magnetic stripe is needed, nor the European Health Insurance Card abroad.
