Support and frequently asked questions

Medipop has no account and no server, so the developer cannot see or recover your data. Here is how to manage the most common cases on your own. Menu names below are given in English; the app shows them in the phone's language (or the one you chose), as written in the English version of the app.

I lost my recovery code

There is no procedure to make a new one, and the developer cannot recover it either: that is what keeps the data private.

  • As long as you have the phone and the app opens, your data is there: you can keep using it normally.
  • If you think you saved it but are not sure, go to More, Security, "Check the code": type or paste the code you found and the app tells you whether it is the right one, without changing anything. Also look for a file named "Medipop-codice-di-recupero.txt" in your password manager, files or cloud.
  • If you share data with the other parent through the family code, backups can also be opened with that code, and the data may also be on their phone.
  • If you lose the code and also the phone (or the key inside it), the data cannot be recovered.

When you can, keep the code somewhere safe (password manager, protected file, paper in a private place).

I have a new phone: how do I move my data?
  1. On the old phone: More, "Backup and family", "Export backup" (or "Share the backup"). Save the .medipop file somewhere you can find it, for example a cloud folder, or send it to yourself.
  2. Keep the old phone's recovery code at hand.
  3. On the new phone: install Medipop (the phone must have a screen lock), then on the welcome screen tap "Restore from a backup". Choose the file and type the old phone's recovery code (or the family code).
  4. Medipop creates a new vault with a new recovery code: save it, because from now on that is the one that counts.

Make the backup before changing phone: if the old one is already gone and you have no backup, the data cannot be recovered.

How do I exchange data with the other parent?
  1. On the first phone: More, "Backup and family", "Create the family code". Send it to the other parent with "Send", or copy it or save it as a file. Best in person or in a different message from the chat where you will send the backups: whoever has the code and a backup can read the data.
  2. On the other phone: More, "Backup and family", "Join a family", "Paste" (or "Load from file").
  3. Now a backup made by one phone opens on the other. With "Share the backup" you send it with the app you prefer (WhatsApp, mail, Drive...); the other opens it with "Open with Medipop" or from "Import a backup".
  4. Before importing you see a preview of what would change. If two profiles look like the same one, the app asks you. Data is merged, not blindly overwritten: for edited records the most recent edit wins.

If you import something by mistake, "Undo last import" puts things back as they were (the safety copy is kept for 7 days).

If the other parent should no longer be able to read the backups: leave the family, create a new code and give it only to whom you want. Backups already saved with the old code remain readable by anyone who knows it.

How do I delete everything?
  • One entry: open it and choose "Delete this entry". A profile with its whole history: from the Family tab, "Delete profile". It cannot be undone.
  • Everything on the phone: uninstall the app. The data is deleted with it.
  • Backups you created stay where you saved or sent them (files, cloud, chats, mail): delete them from there.
  • If you also want the e-mails you sent to the developer deleted, write to medipop.app@gmail.com.
How do I report a problem or make a suggestion?

In the app: More, "Problem or suggestion". Choose the type, write a title and the text. The app opens your mail app with the message already prepared, addressed to medipop.app@gmail.com: read it and send it yourself. Nothing is sent by itself.

The message contains your text and a technical block (app version, system, language, screen name). If you want you can attach a short technical log, which only shows screen names and error types. All details are in the privacy policy.

Do not write health data or names in the message. Describe what you did and what happened, without the contents. If you have no mail app, write to medipop.app@gmail.com.

The app asks me to set a screen lock

Medipop protects your data with a key held by the phone's system and needs the phone to have a PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint (or Face ID on iPhone). Set the lock in the phone's security settings and come back to the app: it checks again by itself.

Does Medipop remind me of medicines? Can it calculate doses?

Reminders, yes: you choose the time and which family members to be reminded for, and a notification never shows the medicine: at most it has the family member's first name, which you can hide in More, Notifications. From version 1.2 a notice also tells you about medicines in the medicine cabinet that are about to expire, again without naming the medicine. Doses, no: Medipop only stores what you type, the dose is the text you copy from the box or the prescription, and the app does not calculate or check it. You cannot save other photos or documents, apart from the pages of medical certificates and the photos of cards (from version 1.1), the pages of prescriptions and the photos of packs (from version 1.2), and it gives no medical advice. It does not check interactions and does not say what a medicine is for. For these things ask your doctor, pharmacist or vet.

Can I keep medical certificates?

Yes, from version 1.1. Medipop has an archive of medical certificates for each person or pet: sport (competitive and non-competitive), sick leave for work, school absence, fitness (work, driving, other) and others. To add one, go to "Add to the diary", "Medical certificate", then add the pages with "Take a photo", "From the gallery" or "Import a PDF". On Android the phone's own camera app takes the picture and Medipop asks for no camera permission; on iPhone it asks the first time you take a photo.

  • The pages are encrypted on the phone and saved without location data.
  • Medipop reads the text on the phone, without internet, and suggests the type, the dates, the absence and the doctor: you confirm or correct each value. The text can be searched in the diary.
  • The expiry notice is optional: the same day or 7, 15, 30 or 60 days before. The notification does not say which certificate it is about: at most it has the family member's first name, which you can hide in More, Notifications.
  • "Share as PDF" builds a PDF of the pages and opens the share sheet. The shared PDF is not encrypted: whoever receives it can read it.
  • The certificate entries are in the encrypted .medipop backups like the other entries. From version 1.1 the backup can also carry the photos of the pages: you choose with a switch.

A copy kept in Medipop does not replace the original document: keep the original. Medipop does not interpret certificates and gives no medical advice.

How do I add a card?

From version 1.1. Go to More, "Cards" and add a card. For the front tap "Take a photo" or "From the gallery"; then do the same for the back, if needed.

Medipop reads the details and the barcode on the phone, without internet, and saves the card straight away. Until you have looked at the details, the card stays marked as still to be checked: open it and check that the details read are right.

How do I show the barcode at the counter?

From version 1.1. Open the card and tap "Show the code": the barcode comes back up, large, and the screen goes to full brightness.

The photo of a card is a handy copy: it does not replace the card where the chip or the magnetic stripe is needed, nor the European Health Insurance Card abroad.

How do I share only one child's data?

From version 1.1, with a circle: the people and pets you choose, in any combination (just one child, two people, even just the dog), shared with someone. You can have several circles, each with its own code and its own choice of whom to share.

  1. Go to More, "Backup and family", "Sharing circles", "Create a circle". Give the circle a name and choose whom to share.
  2. Send the circle's code to the other parent, only once. Better not in the same chat where you will send the backups.
  3. Then tap "Share the backup" (or "Export backup") and choose who the backup is for: the circle. There you can also change who is in it. The backup holds only the people and pets of the circle.
  4. The other parent, in the sharing circles, taps "Join a circle" and pastes the code. Then they open the file with "Open with Medipop".

Whoever has the circle's code sees only the people in that circle, not the rest of the family. If they add or change their entries, when you import their file you see the changes and you confirm them. All the details are in the privacy policy.

The backup with photos is large: how do I save it?

From version 1.1 the backup can also carry the photos of certificates and cards, and the file can become large.

  • With "Share the backup" you choose where to put it: Files, a cloud folder, a message.
  • Or turn the photos off with the switch: the file becomes small and each of you keeps your own photos on your own phone.
How do I read my pet's microchip?

From version 1.1. Open the pet's profile and start editing it. Next to the microchip number, tap the button to read it with the camera and take a photo of the label in the pet passport. Then check the number that was read. The photo is used only to read the number and is not kept.

How do I add a prescription?

From version 1.2. Go to "Add to the diary", "Prescription" and add the pages by taking a photo or importing a PDF.

  • Medipop reads the text and the barcodes on the phone, without internet, and suggests the date, the doctor, the prescription number, the validity and the medicines it recognises. Nothing is added until you confirm.
  • To see the barcode again, large, on the screen, tap "Show the code".
  • From a prescribed medicine, "Create the treatment" opens a treatment already filled in with that medicine. You enter the dose and the times: Medipop does not work them out.
  • You can share the prescription as a PDF. The shared PDF is not encrypted: whoever receives it can read it.
  • Prescriptions, with their pages, are in the encrypted backups like everything else.

It is a personal copy: whether a pharmacy accepts a copy depends on the country and on the kind of prescription. Keep the original.

How do I use the medicine cabinet?

From version 1.2. Go to More, "Medicine cabinet" and add a pack. You can type the details or start from a photo: Medipop reads on the phone, without internet, the small square code printed on the pack (it carries the product, the expiry date and the lot) and, in Italy, also the barcode of the pharmacy sticker; if it finds neither, it tries the printed expiry date. Always check what was read before saving.

  • For each pack: the medicine, whose it is (the whole family, or one person or pet), the expiry date, the lot, how much is left, where you keep it and, if you like, a photo.
  • The list shows expired packs first, then those expiring within 30 days. They also appear in the "Today" tab, under "Expiring".
  • You get one notice 30 days before the expiry and one on the day itself, with a generic text that never names the medicine. You can switch it off for each pack.
  • From a pack, tap "Start a treatment".
  • Packs, with their photo, are in the encrypted backups. A pack that belongs to the whole family is never in a backup made for a circle; the pack of a person or pet in the circle is.
How do I read the package leaflet?

Medipop contains no leaflets and has no internet. For a pack whose code belongs to Italy, Switzerland, Spain, France or to a medicine authorised for the whole EU, open the pack and tap "Package leaflet (official site)": the phone's browser opens on the official site of the medicines agency (AIFA, Swissmedic, AEMPS, the French public medicines database, EMA). Before opening it, Medipop tells you that site will see the page asked for and your IP address, like any site; Medipop sends nothing else.

For German and Austrian codes there is no button, because no official site can be reached from such a code. Medipop does not explain what a medicine is for: ask your doctor or pharmacist.
