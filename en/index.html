Your family's health records, only on your phone

Medipop is a private notebook for the illnesses, visits, treatments and vaccinations of the people and pets in your home. What you write stays on your phone, encrypted. The app does not connect to the internet.

The app is in preparation: store links will be added here when it is published.

What it does

  • Profiles for people and pets, with a family role and a picture of your choice.
  • Illness history, medical visits, treatments (with "Repeat treatment"), vaccinations and medicines bought.
  • A diary with the timeline of everything, and search.
  • App lock with fingerprint or the phone's PIN.
  • Encrypted backups (.medipop files) that you save or send wherever you like, to change phone or to exchange data with the other parent.
  • Available in Italian, English, French, Spanish and German.

How it protects your data

  • No account, no server, no ads, no usage analytics, no tracking.
  • No internet access: the app does not even ask for the permission to connect.
  • The database is encrypted with AES-256. The key is protected by the phone and by a recovery code that only you have: the developer cannot read your data.

If you lose the recovery code and the phone, the data is lost for good. That is why the app asks you to keep the code somewhere safe on first launch.

What it does not do

Medipop only stores what you type. It does not calculate doses, give medical advice or diagnose, and it is not a medical device: it does not replace your doctor or vet. For now there are no notifications or reminders, and you cannot save photos or documents.
