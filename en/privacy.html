Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. The Italian version is the original; this is a translation.

In short: the data you type into Medipop stays on your phone, encrypted. The developer does not receive it, cannot read it and has no server to keep it on. The only thing that can reach the developer is an e-mail that you decide to write.

Who the developer is

Medipop is developed by Vanni M., an individual developer. Contact for privacy and support: privacy@medipop.app.

The data you enter in the app

Medipop stores what you type: people and pets, illnesses, visits, treatments, vaccinations, medicines bought, notes. This is health data, so the app handles it with particular care:

  • it stays only on your phone, in an encrypted database (AES-256, SQLCipher);
  • the app has no internet access (it does not have the permission to connect), no account and no server;
  • there are no usage analytics, ads, tracking or third-party libraries that collect data;
  • the key that opens it is held by the phone's system (Android Keystore, iOS Keychain) and by a recovery code that only you know. The developer has neither the key nor the code.

Because you process this data for purely personal or household purposes (your family's records), the developer is not the one processing it, and data protection law does not apply to that use (Article 2(2)(c) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, GDPR; Article 2(2)(a) of the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, nFADP). The developer does not process personal data of the app's users, except for the e-mails described below.

Children

Medipop is meant for adults. Parents can enter their own children's health data in their own family records, as in a private notebook: the app is not aimed at minors, does not ask for their age and creates no accounts for them.

Backups

A backup is an encrypted .medipop file. You create it and you choose where it goes: the phone's save dialog, or the share sheet (WhatsApp, mail, Drive...). Medipop does not send it to anyone by itself. The file is encrypted: whoever receives it can open it only with Medipop and your recovery code or the family code. From then on the file is under the control of the service you chose (for example your mail or cloud) and its terms; the service can see that the file exists, its size and who you sent it to, but not its contents. Backups you create stay where you put them until you delete them.

The family code lets two parents' phones read each other's backups. Anyone who has the code and a backup can read the data: give it only to people you trust.

Deleting data

  • You can delete a single entry or a whole profile in the app.
  • If you uninstall the app, all data on that phone is deleted.
  • Backups you created stay where you saved or sent them: you have to delete them there.
  • The developer holds no archive of the app's data, so there is nothing to ask the developer to delete.

E-mails you choose to send

In the "More" menu you can choose "Problem or suggestion". Medipop does not send anything by itself: it prepares a message and opens your mail app, and you read it and send it, if you want, to segnalazioni@medipop.app. You can also write to that address with a question without using the app.

What the message prepared by the app contains

  • The subject: "[Medipop version] title", with the title you write (at most 160 characters).
  • The text you write (at most 4000 characters).
  • A block of technical information, which you can see and which stays in the message: type (problem or suggestion), app version and build number, system (Android or iOS) and system version, language, the name of the screen you were on. No data you entered in the app.
  • Only if you tick "Attach the technical log": a short in-memory log (at most the last 3000 characters) with screen names and error types, scrubbed of passwords, e-mail addresses, recovery codes and long digit sequences. The app shows you what will be attached before you send. It contains no health data and nothing you typed into forms.

In addition, like any e-mail, the message arrives with your e-mail address and, if it is set in the account, your name. Your mail provider handles it under its own rules.

Please do not write health data (names, illnesses, medicines, diagnoses) or other personal data in the message, and do not attach files containing them. The message contains whatever you decide to write: if you put health data in it, it reaches the developer. If that happens by mistake, tell us and we will delete it.

What the developer does with e-mails

E-mails received at privacy@medipop.app and segnalazioni@medipop.app
What dataThe sender's e-mail address (and name, if shown), subject, text, technical information and log, any attachments the user sends.
WhyTo reply to the request, to understand and fix the problem, to keep a record of what was asked and solved.
Legal basisReplying to a request from the person concerned, and the legitimate interest in fixing the app's problems (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).
How longUp to 24 months after the last exchange on the subject, then deleted. Earlier on request.
Who receives itNobody. We do not sell or pass on e-mails and do not use them for advertising or to write to people who did not write to us. The @medipop.app addresses go through Cloudflare's forwarding service, which delivers messages without storing them, to a Gmail mailbox used only for Medipop, run by Google.
Transfers outside Italy, the EU or SwitzerlandCloudflare and Google may process e-mails in the United States as well. Both take part in the EU-US and Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Union and Switzerland recognise as an adequate safeguard.

Your rights

For the e-mails you sent us you can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and, where applicable, portability: write to privacy@medipop.app. We will reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority: in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it); in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, edoeb.admin.ch).

Apple and Google

When you download the app, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) process the data of your download and account under their own policies. The developer receives from them, at most, aggregated and anonymous statistics (installs, versions, countries). Medipop sends them no data and receives none from the app.

This website

medipop.app is a website of static pages only, hosted by OVHcloud in France: no cookies, no analytics, no scripts, no content from other sites. Like every web server, OVHcloud's records the IP address and the requested page in its technical logs, for security and to run the service, and keeps them under its own rules. The developer does not use these logs for anything else.

Security

AES-256 encryption of the database and of backup files; keys in the phone's protection system; app lock with fingerprint or PIN; no screenshots while the app is open (on Android) and no preview in recent apps. No system is perfect: on a rooted or jailbroken phone, or if someone knows your recovery code and has one of your backups, the data can be read. If you lose the recovery code and the phone, nobody, not even the developer, can give your data back.

Sources of the medicine list

The list of medicine names inside the app is built from public sources (see the home page). It works without connecting to any service.

Changes to this policy

If something changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. A change in how the app handles data would also be mentioned in the release notes.
